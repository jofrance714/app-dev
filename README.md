# Transformers (2007)

> "Before time began, there was the Cube." — Optimus Prime

---

## 📌 Overview
**Transformers** is a 2007 sci-fi action movie directed by *Michael Bay* and executive produced by *Steven Spielberg*. It brings the classic 1980s toy line to life with groundbreaking visual effects and an epic war between alien robots on Earth.

---

## 🎬 Plot Summary
High school student **Sam Witwicky** buys a 1977 Chevrolet Camaro, only to discover it is actually **Bumblebee**, an alien robot scout. Sam holds the key to locating the **AllSpark**, bringing both the heroic **Autobots** and the villainous **Decepticons** into a high-stakes battle for Earth's survival.

---

## 🤖 Characters

### Autobots
* **Optimus Prime** – Leader of the Autobots
* **Bumblebee** – Scout and Sam's protector
* **Ironhide** – Weapons specialist
* **Ratchet** – Medical officer
* **Jazz** - First Lieutenant and Second-in-Command

### Decepticons
* **Megatron** – Leader of the Decepticons
* **Starscream** – Air commander
* **Barricade** – Scout
* **Brawl** - Weapons specialist and Heavy combatant.
* **Bonecrusher** - Tactician and close-combat Brawler
